## Important SEO/compliance direction

The Australian market is actively protected against commercial academic cheating services. Keep public copy focused on tutoring, proofreading, editing of the student's own work, study guidance, exam preparation and reference/model material. Do not advertise ghostwriting, completed assessments, grade guarantees or submission-ready work.
