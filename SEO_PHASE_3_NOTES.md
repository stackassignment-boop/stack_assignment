## Important SEO rule
These pages are deliberately framed around tutoring, assessment guidance, feedback, editing and study support. They should not promise completed submissions, grade guarantees or other academic-cheating services.

## Data-driven next step

Export these columns from Search Console Performance: query, page, country, clicks, impressions, CTR and position for the last 3 months. Then build the next university/subject pages from queries already earning impressions, especially positions 5–30 and high-impression/low-CTR queries.
